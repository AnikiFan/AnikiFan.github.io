# Homepage beach media

These optimized assets are integrated into this website's homepage cover. They
are not a stock-media download library. Source licenses continue to apply.

- **Video and poster frames:** [Waves coming to the beach, Mixkit #5016](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/waves-coming-to-the-beach-5016/).
  [Mixkit Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree), verified 2026-11-13.
  The 1280 × 720 source was shortened, crossfaded at the loop boundary, and
  encoded as silent H.264 MP4 at 24 fps, with fast-start metadata. The mobile
  version is a central 406 × 720 crop. WebP posters come from the same footage.
- **Audio:** [Beach Ocean Waves](https://opengameart.org/content/beach-ocean-waves)
  by **jasinski**, extracted and submitted by **qubodup** from
  [Alkai Beach / Freesound #18363](https://freesound.org/people/jasinski/sounds/18363/).
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), verified 2026-11-13.
  Four wave excerpts were crossfaded, filtered, attenuated, looped, and encoded
  as a 96 kbps MP3. This is a separate ambient recording, not synchronized
  location audio from the video.

Files in `docs/assets/media/` are served from the same origin as the website;
visitors do not contact the stock-media providers.
